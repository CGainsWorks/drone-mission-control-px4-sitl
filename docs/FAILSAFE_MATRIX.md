# Failsafe matrix

_Generated by `analysis/make_results.py` from the rule table in `src/mission_control/safety/failsafe.py`. Do not edit by hand._

The failsafe logic is a table, not a tree of conditionals. Every row here is a rule object in the source, and `tests/test_safety.py` iterates the same table and drives each row independently — so a rule cannot be added without a test that fires it, and this document cannot drift away from the behaviour.

Rules are listed in the order they resolve. When several fire at once, the highest priority sets the action.

| Priority | Rule | Trigger | Action | Debounce | Why |
| ---: | --- | --- | :---: | ---: | --- |
| 100 | `battery_critical` | battery remaining < 12% | **LAND** | 1.0 s | Below this there is not enough energy to reach home, so descending where it is beats running the pack flat en route. |
| 90 | `gnss_loss` | no usable GNSS for 2.0 s | **LAND** | 0.5 s | This aircraft has no vision or optical-flow fallback, so without GNSS the position estimate dead-reckons on IMU alone and diverges within seconds. RTL is not available because RTL needs a position. |
| 85 | `estimator_uncertain` | EKF horizontal position 1-sigma above its limit | **LAND** | 1.0 s | The fix can be present and the estimate still untrustworthy — after a long dropout, or with the innovation gate rejecting everything. Covariance is the honest test, not fix type. |
| 80 | `geofence_breach` | outside the geofence polygon or altitude band | **RTL** | 0.0 s | No debounce: the position estimate is already filtered, and the fence has a warning margin inside it, so by the time this fires the vehicle has ignored several metres of warning. |
| 70 | `link_loss_critical` | no telemetry for 15 s | **RTL** | 0.0 s | The operator is not coming back. Return home, where the aircraft is recoverable and back in radio range. |
| 60 | `geofence_predicted` | current velocity leads outside the fence within the lookahead | **HOLD** | 0.3 s | Stopping before the boundary rather than after it. HOLD rather than RTL so a mission grazing its own fence is not abandoned outright. |
| 50 | `battery_low` | battery remaining < 25% | **RTL** | 2.0 s | Enough left to fly home with reserve. Debounced because voltage sag under a gust reads as a lower state of charge than is real. |
| 40 | `link_loss` | no telemetry for 3 s | **HOLD** | 0.0 s | Hold position and wait. Most link losses are brief — an antenna null on a turn — and abandoning a mission for one is expensive. |

## Actions

| Action | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `none` | Nothing wrong. |
| `warn` | Advisory only; the mission continues. |
| `hold` | Stop and hold position at the current point. |
| `rtl` | Climb to the RTL altitude, transit home, descend, land. |
| `land` | Descend and land where the vehicle is. |
| `terminate` | Cut thrust. Reserved for cases where continued flight is more dangerous than falling. |

## Two deliberate behaviours

**Actions latch and escalate, never de-escalate.** A vehicle that began returning home on a low battery does not resume its mission because the voltage recovered — that recovery is what every battery does when the load drops, and acting on it would send the aircraft back out on a pack that cannot make it. Escalation is allowed: a low-battery RTL becomes a critical-battery LAND.

**Conditions must persist.** Every rule carries a debounce, so one dropped telemetry packet or one noisy GNSS epoch cannot trigger a return. The geofence breach rule is the exception at 0.0 s, because the position estimate feeding it is already filtered and the fence has a warning margin inside it — by the time it fires, several metres of warning have been ignored.

## Coverage

`tests/test_runner.py` drives each of these end to end through a complete simulated flight, not just against a synthetic status struct: telemetry is actually silenced, the battery is actually drained, the GNSS actually stops producing fixes, and the geofence is actually breached.
