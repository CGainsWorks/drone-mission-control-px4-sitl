# Results

_Generated by `analysis/make_results.py` from `results/runs.csv`. Do not edit by hand._

84 runs: 42 scenarios flown by both controllers, paired on identical seeds so the wind, IMU noise and GNSS errors are sample-for-sample identical between the two.

All errors are against **ground truth**, over the **mission phase only** (takeoff, RTL and landing are excluded; they are near-vertical and would dilute the comparison).

## Headline

- **40 of 42 paired scenarios go to the MPC**, with a median error ratio of 1.23x.
- Mean RMS position error: **PID 0.9073 m**, **MPC 0.7095 m**.
- Mean cross-track error: PID 0.2921 m, MPC 0.2301 m.
- Mean RMS jerk of the acceleration demand: PID 25.12 m/s^3, MPC 17.08 m/s^3.

## Scenarios beyond the airframe

These scenarios spend a large fraction of the mission at the tilt or acceleration limit, which means the *reference* is asking for more than the aircraft can deliver. The error there is mostly a property of the trajectory, not of the controller, and the two controllers converge because both are simply saturated.

They are kept in the grid deliberately — a comparison that only includes cases the vehicle can fly comfortably would overstate how much the choice of controller matters — but the numbers should be read with that in mind.

| Scenario | PID saturated | MPC saturated | PID RMS | MPC RMS |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `corridor survey` fast/gusty | 51% | 37% | 4.8897 m | 4.8332 m |
| `corridor survey (sharp corners)` nominal/calm | 24% | 5% | 1.2719 m | 0.1738 m |
| `corridor survey` nominal/gusty | 21% | 17% | 0.7475 m | 0.7415 m |
| `corridor survey (sharp corners)` nominal/gusty | 43% | 22% | 1.7444 m | 0.7631 m |
| `tower orbit` fast/gusty | 36% | 24% | 1.5583 m | 1.4600 m |

## Where the difference comes from

- **Feasible reference (rounded corners)** — 34/36 to the MPC, median ratio 1.21x (PID 0.8071 m, MPC 0.7497 m).
- **Infeasible reference (sharp corners)** — 6/6 to the MPC, median ratio 4.80x (PID 1.5082 m, MPC 0.4685 m).
- **Fast settings only** — 16/18 to the MPC, median ratio 1.16x (PID 1.2727 m, MPC 1.2146 m).
- **Gusty conditions only** — 19/21 to the MPC, median ratio 1.04x (PID 1.4160 m, MPC 1.2322 m).

## What it costs

| Controller | Optimisation / control law | Whole control step | 99th percentile step |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| PID | 358.0 us | 358.0 us | 1499.3 us |
| MPC | 1073.3 us | 4462.2 us | 12190.7 us |

The control period at 50 Hz is 20 000 us, so both are affordable. The optimisation column is the QP alone; the whole-step column adds sampling the reference across the horizon, which is real work but is cacheable in a way the QP is not.

**These four numbers are the only ones on this page that do not reproduce.** Every flight-derived figure above comes out bit-identical on a re-run, because the simulation is seeded; these are wall-clock measurements of this machine under whatever else it was doing at the time. Re-running the pipeline on a busy machine moved the means by about 1.5x and the 99th percentile by 2.1x, with every other number on the page unchanged. Read them as an order of magnitude against the 20 000 us budget, not as a benchmark, and do not treat a difference from these values as a regression.
